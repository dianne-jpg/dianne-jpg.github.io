About

I spent close to a decade in natural resources — habitat restoration, wildland fire, and park ranger work. Hands-on, physical, often working off field data and tools like Avenza and GIS.

Now I'm teaching myself to build the kind of technology I used to just operate: embedded systems, sensors, the electronics behind interactive objects. I'm also picking up cybersecurity and networking fundamentals. I don't have years of code behind me yet. What I've got is ideas, a systems-thinking brain from the field, and enough stubbornness to keep soldering until the thing actually works.

Kingdom Hearts Music Box

A 3D-printed, Paopu-themed box that lights up and plays music, built by soldering a sound module to a set of LEDs, with magnets and a reed switch to trigger it on open. My first hardware project — built with no prior 3D printing, soldering, or engineering experience, and the one that proved I could take an idea from CAD file to a working physical object.

  • 3D printing
  • Soldering
  • Sound module
  • LEDs

Proximity Wayfinders

3D-printed Kingdom Hearts Wayfinders that will glow brighter the closer together they get, using ESP32-C3 boards and Bluetooth proximity sensing. Sketch skeleton and LED/PWM setup are in place; wiring and on-hardware testing are the current step, with Bluetooth proximity sensing coming next.

  • ESP32-C3
  • Arduino IDE
  • Bluetooth (BLE)
